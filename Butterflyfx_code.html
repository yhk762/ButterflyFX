What This Is About

"Butterfly Effect" --It's been said that something as small as the flutter of a butterfly's wing can cause a typhoon halfway around the world. History is filled with such linchpins-- small events or decisions that have huge effects on the future. Make you own change somewhere in history and show us the effects on the world.

This story draws on my experience as a UAC student preparing to move to SLC. The branching events and future scenarios are imagined possibilities, not predictions.
